About Nextasy
Vision

To become a trusted global aviation consulting partner.

Mission

To deliver practical, innovative and data-driven aviation solutions that create sustainable business value.

Nextasy Aviation Consulting Pvt. Ltd. is a professional aviation consulting company providing strategic, operational and commercial solutions to the aviation and travel industry.

We work alongside airlines, airports, travel businesses and aviation investors to improve performance, strengthen operations, identify opportunities and prepare for future growth — grounded in route economics, network realities and on-the-ground operational detail rather than generic strategy frameworks.

Our consultants bring backgrounds across airline planning, airport operations and travel commercial strategy, and apply that experience to problems that are specific to each client's fleet, network and market position.
